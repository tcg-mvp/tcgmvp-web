# Current Priority

## Active Phase

**Phase 4 – Market Intelligence Platform**

TCGMVP has moved beyond the initial live-pipeline stage, the initial sold-data integration stage, and the initial three-product validation catalog.

The platform now combines three distinct forms of market evidence:

1. Historical/reference pricing from TCGPlayer/TCGCSV

2. Live active-supply evidence from the eBay Browse API

3. Completed-sale evidence from SoldComps

These sources now feed the quantitative analytics architecture rather than relying on manually entered sales or placeholder evidence.

TCGMVP also now has a scalable catalog-onboarding architecture and broad modern English booster-box coverage.

The primary remaining Phase 4 gaps are **unattended automation, expanded analytics validation, production monitoring, long-term proprietary historical accumulation, cross-source agreement scoring, and advanced market-depth intelligence**.

## Immediate Next Steps

1. Complete and regression-test manifest-driven batch validation and batch promotion.

2. Push and preserve the expanded 34-product modern English booster-box catalog and catalog-automation baseline.

3. Automate scheduled TCGCSV and eBay collection.

4. Improve SoldComps quota handling and production scheduling around provider limits.

5. Add production-grade import-run logging, failure monitoring, and provider-status reporting.

6. Validate analytics across the expanded 34-product catalog, including high-liquidity, low-liquidity, overvalued, undervalued, high-risk, and conflicting-signal cases.

7. Add generalized outlier detection and improve marketplace title/product-type filtering.

8. Accumulate daily historical listing, supply, price, and sold evidence to build proprietary longitudinal datasets.

9. Add cross-source pricing comparison and agreement scoring.

10. Add historical active-supply and sold-volume intelligence.

11. Complete Phase 3D regression testing and contradiction detection across the expanded catalog.

12. Prepare for private beta once unattended automation and expanded analytics validation are stable.
